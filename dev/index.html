A player below the bar still counts if he's due: playing the minutes, creating chances like a top-quarter player at his position, with a good next 2. Cup and European games

Minutes your players and watch list play outside the league: Champions League, Europa League, Conference League, FA Cup and League Cup. Three things to look for. Rotation: a full 90 midweek a few days before a league game makes a rest more likely, especially for clubs playing twice a week. Pecking order: managers rest their first choices in the domestic cups, so a league starter left out is a good sign, and a fringe player starting the cup is usually the backup. Fitness: real minutes in a cup game after an injury mean he's ready for the league. Goals in these games don't count toward your tests; they're context only.
